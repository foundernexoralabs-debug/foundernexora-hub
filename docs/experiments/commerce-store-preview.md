# Commerce experiment — preview-first, no-subscription gate (2026-09-29)

**Status:** research and visual-preview experiment only. Do not claim a store is live or revenue has been earned.

## Brief
Develop a distinctive, polished Shopify storefront for useful desk accessories and forward-looking technology tools for students, developers, remote workers and creators. Start with a tight selection and an ownable, coherent brand. The store must be separate from the FounderNexora/Renor product identity, while its operational performance may later be visible inside a consent-based Renor Shopify connector.

## Preview gate
1. Generate up to three Shopify-native *new-store* previews and review mobile hero, collections, product page, cart, product narrative, accessibility and brand differentiation. A generated preview is not transferable to an already existing Shopify store.
2. Select a direction; prepare a concise creative brief and make requested changes through available tooling or a reviewable prototype. Do not claim the preview has been modified in Shopify unless the tool actually supports it.
3. Research at least three defensible product candidates: real supplier evidence, landed cost including tax, shipping/returns, truthful delivery estimates, original media, buyer need and competition. No 'winning product' claim without evidence.
4. Prototype a hero offer (possibly a desk-upgrade bundle) with transparent sourcing, accurate details and realistic mobile checkout.
5. Validate with honest low-cost demand tests. Family test orders, if any, are marked internal fulfilment tests, never portrayed as independent market traction.

## Spending gate — default: £0 until owner approval
- No paid Shopify plan, app, domain, media purchase, inventory or advertising without explicit owner sign-off and a current budget.
- Read the offer terms *at checkout*. A third-party partner transfer may invalidate new-customer introductory offers.
- Isolate store finances, books, customer data and permissions from Renor.
- Every investment decision includes expected unit economics, break-even order count, possible refunds and worst-case cash loss. Stop or revise if conversion and gross contribution fail predefined tests.
- If proceeding with UK sales, verify trader disclosures, tax, privacy, cancellation and returns requirements before collecting payments.

## Product page quality bar
Clear 1-sentence promise; measured benefits, real specifications and provenance; transparent delivery, returns and contact details; verified product imagery; fast mobile experience; accessible motion; no fake countdowns, scarcity, reviews or testimonials.

## Later Renor integration — only when justified
Read-only store health and analytics first; Shopify OAuth or approved app authorization, least-privilege scopes, encrypted credentials and revocation. Show evidence-backed order and sales receipts in chat, with fresh timestamps and consent. Every write action (products, discounts, refunds, inventory) needs specific user approval, idempotency and audit logs. Do not expose customer personal data to other products or memory scopes.
